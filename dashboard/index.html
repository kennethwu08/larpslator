Your Voice

Translations are spoken in a clone of your voice. Read the text below at your normal pace in a quiet room: about a minute, at least 20 seconds.

Hi, I'm setting up my voice for Larpslator. I'll read this out loud at a normal pace, the way I usually talk. On weekends I play pickleball and tennis with friends; we argue about the kitchen line, the score, and whose serve was out. During the week I study computer science and math: algorithms, proofs, matrices, and the occasional bug that takes all night to find. Honestly, the best part of any day is a good conversation, a quick laugh, and maybe a coffee afterwards. Thanks for listening. This should be enough for the voice to sound like me.
