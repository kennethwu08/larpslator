## Files and reproduction

- papers.jsonl: six records with source_language, summary_language, provenance, access status, and human-review flags.
- licensed_fulltext/: four English XML articles and four attributed text extractions. Original CC BY notices remain in XML and text headers.
- candidates.json: uncollected leads and actual access failures.
- collection_report.json: exact downloaded source URLs, timestamps, license checks, metadata, XML hashes, and outcomes.
- collect_papers.py: standard-library collector; fetches only the DOI-selected open-access articles through Europe PMC. TLS verification remains enabled. No login walls or anti-bot challenges are bypassed.
- search_log.json: searches used and the Tamil sports literature gap.

Run from the repository root:

~~~powershell
python data/research/tamil/collect_papers.py
~~~

The collector refreshes the four English source texts and records metadata-only status for the Tamil records. It does not translate papers or regenerate summaries. Read failure and license fields before counting items as collected.

## Scope and use

The 2023 Tamil IT-term comparison is the most directly relevant lead for jargon-aware translation. The 2015 internet-history paper is contextual, not a modern algorithms or systems paper. Three other computing leads remain unverified or inaccessible and are listed separately.

Sports articles are research sources, not on-court bilingual terminology dictionaries. They cover biomechanics and wellbeing; they do not validate preferred Tamil equivalents for kitchen, dink, love, or break point. Do not treat the Tamil summaries as a training set or gold-standard translation evaluation.

The IT-term paper's findings were not inspected. Its Tamil note describes the subject only. The 2015 abstract includes historical assertions that were not independently fact-checked.

No full Tamil journal text is redistributed: full-body access was not established and redistribution permission was not verified. Direct links are provided for reading. All four saved English articles passed explicit CC BY license-link checks.

This collection is separate from the earlier Korean glossaries. Those files remain Korean; do not pass a *-ko.json glossary when translating into Tamil. No application defaults, classifier labels, or secrets were changed.

Application implementation is outside the research scope. See ../../TEAM_HANDOFF_TAMIL.md
for the handoff. No translation runner is included in this contribution.
